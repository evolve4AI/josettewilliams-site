Josette Williams

Founder · Identity Architect · Identity Infrastructure Innovator

Through her framework The New Feminine Economies, she develops the infrastructure and proprietary frameworks — including Self-Licensing and the Self-License Token — that let women own, govern, and license their identity as economic leverage in the AI economy.

She is the originator of the Living Identity OS (formerly the Distributed Identity Engine), and of the connected body of frameworks defined in the Emhergence Lexicon.
